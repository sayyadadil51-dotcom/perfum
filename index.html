Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can Rani Kothi Lawns accommodate?
The garden lawn comfortably hosts up to 800 guests, and the banquet hall up to 400. Booking both spaces together works beautifully for multi-function weddings.
Do you provide catering and decoration?
Yes — in-house catering with menu tasting, plus full decoration (mandap, stage, floral décor and lighting). Guests rate the food as “really worth tasting”.
Is there parking at the venue?
Yes, ample parking space is available on-site — one of the most praised facilities in our reviews.
How do I check if my date is free?
Submit the booking form below or call 098231 70071 — we’ll confirm availability the same day. Checking availability is free with no charges.
Can I visit the venue before booking?
Absolutely. Walk in any day until 9 pm, or book a slot through the form so our team can show you around the lawn and hall personally.
Where exactly is the venue located?
237, Temple Rd, Civil Lines, Nagpur, Maharashtra 440001 (Plus Code 5328+8Q) — right in the heart of Civil Lines with easy access across the city.
