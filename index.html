FAQ

Good questions, quick answers

How far in advance should I book?

Popular wedding dates (November–February weekends) are usually taken 6–12 months ahead. In the off-season, 4–8 weeks' notice often works. Call us — we sometimes have last-minute openings.

What are the session timings?

Morning sessions run 9 AM–2 PM and evening sessions 4 PM–9 PM. Full-day exclusivity is available on request. Site visits are welcome daily, 9:00 AM to 7:30 PM.

Can we bring our own caterer or decorator?

We offer in-house catering and a curated panel of decorators so quality never varies — menus are fully customisable. For special requirements, talk to our team and we'll work something out.

Is parking really available for 250+ cars?

Yes — covered and open parking for over 250 cars, with valet assistance for wedding parties. Your guests never circle the block in Khamla.

How is a booking confirmed?

Send a request here or call 096899 02501. We confirm availability within 24 hours, then a token advance blocks your date — balance as per an agreed schedule.

What is the cancellation policy?

We're flexible. Advance is refundable (minus incurred expenses) when cancelled 60+ days before the event. Closer dates are handled case by case — talk to us early and we'll be fair.
